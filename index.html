HOW TO READ A REPORT
01
What was observed

Cart, tent, walking off, or a return — only if the linked report actually says so.

02
That game's outcome

“Out” and “returned” refer to the listed game, never automatically to next week.

03
Later outlook

Updates keep the source date and wording. An expected absence is not a confirmed diagnosis or return date.

i
Severity is not a score.

We do not infer clinical severity from a cart, a blue tent, or a scoreboard. Unconfirmed means unconfirmed.

03 / THE STANDARD

A clear trail from
source to signal.

We begin with league-published reporting, match a player and club to an actual game, and quote the precise line behind every claim. Ambiguous identity, conflicting wording, missing metadata, or a missing source means “hold,” not “guess.”

Official pregame injury report ↗
01In-game first

While a game is live the page polls a partner header and news lane every 20–45 seconds and shows only wording that explicitly says a player will not return, is questionable, was carted off, or went to the tent. It is labelled UNVERIFIED and carries the provider's own timestamp.

02Cross-check

For the verified list, a player link, club, game date and an explicit league-or-club status sentence must all align. Otherwise it goes to the review queue, not the feed.

03Publish with context and tier

Every lane is labelled official, partner or unofficial, and the archive only keeps official grounding. A lane that cannot be reached says so instead of going quiet.
