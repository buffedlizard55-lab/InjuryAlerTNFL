HOW TO READ A REPORT
01
What was observed

Cart, tent, walking off, or a return — only if the linked report actually says so.

02
That game's outcome

“Out” and “returned” refer to the listed game, never automatically to next week.

03
Later outlook

Updates keep the source date and wording. An expected absence is not a confirmed diagnosis or return date.

i
Severity is not a score.

We do not infer clinical severity from a cart, a blue tent, or a scoreboard. Unconfirmed means unconfirmed.

03 / THE STANDARD

A clear trail from
source to signal.

We begin with league-published reporting, match a player and club to an actual game, and quote the precise line behind every claim. Ambiguous identity, conflicting wording, missing metadata, or a missing source means “hold,” not “guess.”
