HOW AN ALERT REACHES YOU

Fast lanes,
labelled tiers.

A game in progress is polled every 10–20 seconds directly from this page. Official club pages are read continuously by the repository's own scanner, and every result is written to the log with the sentence it came from.

Notifications work only while this page is open. RSS is also available ↗

HOW TO READ A REPORT
01
What was observed

Cart, tent, walking off, or a return — only if the linked report actually says so.

02
That game's outcome

“Out” and “returned” refer to the listed game, never automatically to next week.

03
Later outlook

Updates keep the source date and wording. An expected absence is not a confirmed diagnosis or a return date.

i
Severity is not a score.

We do not infer clinical severity from a cart, a blue tent, or a scoreboard. Unconfirmed means unconfirmed, and an empty lane is never an all-clear.

07 / THE STANDARD

A clear trail from
source to signal.

We begin with league-published reporting, match a player and club to an actual game, and quote the precise line behind every claim. Ambiguous identity, conflicting wording, missing metadata, or a missing source means “hold,” not “guess.”

01In-game first

While a game is live the page polls the game lanes every 10–20 seconds and shows only wording that explicitly says a player will not return, is questionable, was carted off, or went to the tent. It is labelled with its tier and carries the provider's own timestamp.

02Two independent reads

Automatic promotion from a club page needs the identical sentence read twice, in two separate scans, plus a resolvable game date and the club named in the sentence. Anything less stays in the auto-matched list with the failed gate printed.

03Publish with context and tier

Every lane is labelled official, partner or unofficial — and also by category, latency class, timestamp precision and access — and the archive only keeps official grounding. A lane that cannot be reached says so instead of going quiet.
