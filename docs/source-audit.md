# Source audit — 101 checked in-game reports

**Checked September 24, 2026 (20 entries from the first audit, 20 from the second, 20 from the third pass, 34 from the merged fourth pass, and 8 from a fifth expansion pass — 101 total, after one Pass-4 row was withdrawn during the fifth pass's re-check — all in the same verification window).** This is a selected historical sample, not a live all-NFL injury report. The links below go to NFL.com or official club game reporting and dated follow-ups; each claim also has a short excerpt in [the master ledger](../data/archive.json) and on the website. NFL.com editorial coverage is not interchangeable with the league's pregame Game Status Report. Do not infer medical severity or next-game availability from an in-game outcome.

Every excerpt was re-verified line by line against the live source page text on September 24, 2026 (217 claims across 49 official article URLs, after four redundant claims that repeated another claim's exact source sentence were dropped). A second re-check the same day found nine *older* excerpts whose pages had changed under them: five stored a nested quotation with ASCII single quotes where the page renders typographic double quotes, one dropped a possessive, one omitted a `(ankle; DNP)` parenthetical, one crossed an inline-link boundary, and one sentence was trimmed out of a rolling roundup altogether. Each of the eight re-groundable cases now quotes the sentence the page serves, and the ninth became item 31 below. `scripts/verify_sources.py` now prints the nearest live sentence next to any excerpt it cannot find, and the CI job files one annotation per drifted claim, so this class of problem is listed in full instead of being truncated to three lines. Four pre-existing excerpts found during earlier re-checks were corrected in place: three because their wording crossed an inline link boundary, and the Dart coach-confirmation sentence because NFL.com reworded the article after publication. Club newsrooms (Packers, Buccaneers, Jets, Colts, Seahawks, Jaguars, Cardinals, Chargers, Commanders, Cowboys, Broncos, Bills, etc.) and /videos/ paths are allowed as official sources per `scripts/schema.py` and `assets/app.js`. The schema also accepts the club-recap phrase "missed the remainder of the game" as did_not_return grounding.
