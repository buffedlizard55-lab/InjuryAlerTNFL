## Method

Each row was checked against the linked official league or club article at the claim level. Publication date and game date are different fields. Later expectations (“expected,” “will be placed”) are not marked as completed actions. The automated collector uses narrower rules than this manually audited seed; it never upgrades a held identity/body-part discrepancy on its own.
