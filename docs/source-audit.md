## Method

Each row was checked against the linked official league or club article at the claim level, and every excerpt was re-fetched and re-compared line by line on September 23, 2026. Publication date and game date are different fields. Later expectations ("expected," "will be placed") are not marked as completed actions; completed roster moves (injured reserve) are only marked when a source states them as done. The automated collector uses narrower rules than this manually audited seed; it never upgrades a held identity/body-part discrepancy on its own. [Read the schema and limitations](../README.md#verification-and-language-rules).
