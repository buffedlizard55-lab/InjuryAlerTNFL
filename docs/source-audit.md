The first 123 excerpts were re-verified line by line against live source page text on September 23, 2026. The 31 excerpts added in the fourth pass were checked the same day against the official page text as fetched; they were not invented. A direct TLS connection to www.nfl.com from this environment fails, so the online CI re-check remains the confirmation path for those URLs. Four pre-existing excerpts found during those re-checks were corrected in place: three because their wording crossed an inline link boundary (a possessive or comma directly attached to a linked phrase, where the page's visible-text join inserts a space) — the Mike Evans and Avonte Maddox Monday-report quotes and the Jonathon Brooks surgery quote, which now quote only the contiguous sentence fragment each URL plainly states — and the Dart coach-confirmation sentence because NFL.com reworded the article after publication (it now reads "confirmed on Wednesday afternoon Dart will undergo season-ending surgery"); the ledger quotes the sentence as served, a change caught by the CI re-check using [scripts/diagnose_audit.py](../scripts/diagnose_audit.py). ## Method

Each row was checked against the linked official league or club article at the claim level. Publication date and game date are different fields. Later expectations ("expected," "will be placed") are not marked as completed actions; completed roster moves (injured reserve) are only marked when a source states them as done. A phrase such as "cleared to return," "came back into the game," or "first play back" is not recorded as `returned` unless the excerpt contains "returned" or "returning." "Carted off the field" is not recorded as "Carted to locker room." One player's outcome marker in a shared sentence is not applied to another player. The automated collector uses narrower rules than this manually audited seed; it never upgrades a held identity or body-part discrepancy on its own.
